How the card is built

Sentinel-1 SAR gives annual water extent (Otsu threshold). Sentinel-2 gives NDSI, NDTI and NDCI, Landsat 8/9 gives lake surface temperature (LST) and ERA5-Land gives 2 m air temperature, all for the October–December post-monsoon window. Five indicators are min–max normalised, weighted with the Entropy Weight Method and ranked with TOPSIS. The closeness coefficient is the HAWHC Index (HAWHCI). Wetland area is shown beside the index and is not part of it.

Health classes

Classes are relative to these five wetlands. Excellent means best of the five, not an absolute ecological verdict.

Satellite image for

Add a Sentinel-2 or other satellite image of the selected wetland, for example a PNG or JPG exported from Earth Engine. Enter its bounds in decimal degrees (EPSG:4326). It then shows in the Boundary view, under the lake outline.

Load your data

Until a CSV is loaded, the page shows only the values reported in the manuscript: scores, weights, value ranges and a few single-year points. Loading a CSV adds full time series and recomputes the weights and scores in your browser. Loaded files stay in this browser only.
